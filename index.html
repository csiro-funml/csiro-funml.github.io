Research

Four directions,
one principle: uncertainty matters.

Our methods come with guarantees and work on real scientific problems, where data is scarce, experiments are expensive and decisions carry weight.

01

Causal AI

Moving from correlation to cause: discovering causal structure from data, quantifying uncertainty over causal graphs, and bringing human experts into the loop.

02

Bayesian Optimisation & Experimental Design

Choosing the next experiment wisely, with generative and variational search, Thompson sampling in function spaces, adaptive calibration of simulators, and theory for neural surrogates.

03

Uncertainty Quantification & Probabilistic Inference

Knowing what a model doesn't know, with new variational objectives, fractional posteriors, robust neural processes and scalable approximate Bayesian inference.

04

Scientific Machine Learning

Learning the operators behind physical systems, from turbulent flows and shallow water to global climate, with multi-scale architectures built for long, stable rollouts.
