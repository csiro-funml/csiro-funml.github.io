Fundamental Machine Learning

We develop principled methods for learning and decision-making under uncertainty, from Bayesian optimisation and probabilistic inference to operator learning and causal AI.

Explore our code GitHub

Research

Our work spans theory and practice, with a focus on methods that come with guarantees and scale to real scientific problems.

Bayesian optimisation

Generative and variational approaches, Thompson sampling with neural operators, and theory for nonlinear models.

Probabilistic inference

Variational learning of fractional posteriors, Rényi neural processes and search distributions.

Operator learning

Multi-scale wavelet transformers for learning the dynamics of physical systems.

Causal AI

Causal preference elicitation, climate attribution and decision intelligence.

Experimental design

Bayesian adaptive calibration, optimal design and active learning.
